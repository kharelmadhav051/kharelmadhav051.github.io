Research

At the Zhang Lab (Research Foundation for SUNY), I contribute to multiple federally and state-funded projects on the environmental fate, transport, and sustainable remediation of organic contaminants.

NSF Project II — PFAS Bioaccumulation in Edible Crops

2025–2028 · Ongoing

Investigating PFAS bioaccumulation behavior, uptake mechanisms, and food quality implications in edible crops.

U.S. EPA Project — PFAS in Land-Applied Biosolids

2024–2027 · Ongoing

Building a mechanistic understanding of PFAS fate and mitigation in agricultural settings where biosolids are land-applied.

NSF Project I — ERASE-PFAS

2022–2025 · Completed

Stabilization of per- and polyfluorinated substances in sewage sludge intended for land application.

NYS DEC Project — PFAS Risk in the Great Lakes Basin

2024–2025 · Completed

Assessing and mitigating PFAS contamination risks in surface water resulting from biosolids land application.

Key contributions

  • Investigating PFAS–plant interaction mechanisms to understand bioaccumulation and translocation in edible crops.
  • Developing sustainable remediation and detection techniques, including phytoremediation, sorption, stabilization, and electrochemical sensing.
  • Conducting environmental risk assessment and contaminant analysis in aqueous and solid waste matrices.
  • Assisting in data analysis, report writing, and scientific manuscript preparation.
